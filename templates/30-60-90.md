# 30/60/90 Plan Template

Fill-in-the-blank template for the GTH 580 first-90-days module (M4). One page. The 30/60/90 is the arrival document that gets you hired and sets you up: **30 days — learn**, **60 days — own**, **90 days — drive**. It names the people you'll learn from, the feature you'll own, the metric you'll move, and the risks you'll watch. It shows you arrive already doing the job.

**Role:** [TITLE — AI PRODUCT MANAGER] · **Company / team:** [COMPANY — TEAM] · **Start date:** [DATE] · **Prepared by:** [NAME]

---

## 30 Days — Learn

Make no changes yet. The goal is to understand the system before touching it: the business, the users, the metrics, and the model stack. Interview everyone.

| Person (by role) | What I'll learn from them |
|---|---|
| [ENGINEERING LEAD — model stack, latency budget, deployment pipeline] | [MODEL VERSIONS, CACHING, EVAL INFRA, WHERE THE BODIES ARE BURIED] |
| [DATA / ANALYTICS LEAD — metrics definitions, instrumentation] | [NORTH STAR, GUARDRAILS, WHICH METRICS LIE AND HOW (metric failure modes)] |
| [DESIGN / UX — user workflows] | [WHO USES WHAT, THE JOURNEY, WHERE USERS STRUGGLE] |
| [SALES / SUPPORT — what customers actually ask for] | [REAL REQUESTS VS. THE ROADMAP; SUPPORT TICKETS AS EVAL CASES] |
| [LEGAL / COMPLIANCE — PII, consent, governance] | [WHAT DATA WE MAY USE, RETENTION, EU AI ACT RISK CLASS OF OUR FEATURES] |
| [HEAD OF PRODUCT — priorities, politics, what "good" means here] | [THE PRIORITIES, THE DECISION PROCESS, WHAT EARNS TRUST] |
| [PEER PMs — how work gets done here] | [PROCESS, TOOLS, WHO TO INVOLVE WHEN] |

## 60 Days — Own

Take one small feature end-to-end — discovery, spec, ship, measure. A completed loop builds more trust than a hundred good opinions. Scope it small, completable, measurable.

- **The feature:** [NAME — ONE SMALL SLICE, e.g. "surface source links on answers," NOT "the new assistant platform"]
- **Why this one:** [SMALL ENOUGH TO SHIP IN ~4 WEEKS, MEASURABLE, VISIBLE, AND IT LEVERAGES MY STRENGTH — e.g. eval discipline]
- **Definition of done:** [THE SHIP + THE MEASUREMENT — e.g. live for ___% of traffic with the guardrail metrics instrumented]
- **First contribution:** [ADAPT THE PRODUCTION EVAL PLAN (`production-eval-plan.md`) TO THEIR STACK — THE EVAL-AND-RISK POSTURE FOR MY PRODUCT AREA]

## 90 Days — Drive

Move a metric with evidence. Propose the roadmap. Establish the eval-and-risk posture as the standing practice.

- **The metric I will move:** [NAMED METRIC WITH BASELINE — e.g. "deflection rate from 31% → 40% for tier-1 queries," or "p95 latency under budget"] — the metric must have a definition, an owner (me), and a stated failure mode
- **The evidence plan:** [HOW I PROVE THE MOVE — experiment per DATA 530: pre-registered hypothesis, guardrails as primary outcomes, novelty-effect check]
- **Roadmap contribution:** [THE ONE THING I'LL ARGUE FOR NEXT, WITH THE EVAL DATA TO BACK IT]
- **Standing posture:** [WEEKLY EVAL RUN, REGRESSION GATE ENFORCED, DRIFT SIGNALS MONITORED, FEEDBACK LOOP CURATED — THE QUALITY CONVERSATION HAS AN OWNER: ME]

## Risks to Watch

|---|---|---|
