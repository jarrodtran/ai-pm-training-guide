Self-grade (after reading the teaching note): [A / B / C] — [ONE LINE: what the teaching note caught that your memo missed, or what your memo did better.]

## Done when

- [ ] The memo fits on one page
- [ ] The position is stated in the first paragraph
- [ ] Every factual claim carries a source and a source type (research / vendor / independent)
- [ ] Counter-evidence and missing evidence are addressed, not hidden
- [ ] At least one framework is named and actually used, not just mentioned
- [ ] Risks have mitigations, and assumptions are stated explicitly
- [ ] The recommendation names the metric that will show it worked
- [ ] The self-grade line is filled in after reading the teaching note
